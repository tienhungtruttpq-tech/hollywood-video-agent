The next icons prove that action can take many forms. Samuel L Jackson can turn a few lines into a defining movie moment, from Pulp Fiction to Nick Fury and beyond. His voice, timing, and presence make even an ensemble feel larger. Hugh Jackman brought musical theater energy and fierce physical commitment to Wolverine, then showed another side in The Greatest Showman. Sigourney Weaver changed science fiction with Ripley in Alien, a character defined by intelligence and survival rather than easy stereotypes. Ghostbusters and Avatar later displayed her comedy and imagination. Six action careers, each built on a different kind of strength. Which hero or heroine would you add to the next chapter?
